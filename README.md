# jupyter-exploration
This project explores how computer vision is used in autonomous vehicles (self-driving cars). Autonomous vehicles use cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, sensors, and artificial intelligence to understand their surroundings and make safe driving decisions.

Computer vision acts as the vehicle’s eyes, helping it detect and recognize pedestrians, vehicles, traffic lights, road signs, lanes, and other objects. Technologies such as object detection, lane detection, object tracking, and sensor fusion allow the vehicle to understand its environment. AI then uses this information to predict what may happen next and decide when to accelerate, brake, stop, or turn.

The project also discusses the benefits and challenges of autonomous vehicles. Major benefits include potentially improving road safety, reducing accidents caused by human error, and providing transportation for people who cannot drive. Challenges include difficult weather conditions, unexpected road situations, cybersecurity risks, privacy concerns, accident responsibility, and public trust.

Overall, autonomous vehicles demonstrate how computer vision can move beyond simply recognizing images to making real-time decisions in the physical world. As AI and sensor technology continue to improve, autonomous vehicles may become safer and more capable, while also creating important social, ethical, and employment-related questions.
